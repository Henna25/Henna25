About

I am a researcher with an M.Sc. in Oceanography from Cochin University of Science and Technology and a background in physics. My work combines numerical ocean modelling with data-driven forecasting — from evaluating how ROMS and MOM6 represent the vertical temperature and salinity structure of the Indian Ocean, to building hybrid machine-learning models for ENSO and sea surface salinity prediction.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in ocean–climate interactions and machine learning for oceanic prediction.
